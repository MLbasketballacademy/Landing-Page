Welcome to MLBA

To provide a safe and supportive basketball community where kids of all ages can develop athletic abilities through discipline training and high level coaching.

Our Mission

MLBA exists to provide Moses Lake youth with a professional basketball development program focused on fundamentals, leadership, and sportsmanship.

Through strong coaching, organized training, and community support, we aim to build one of the premier youth basketball programs in the region.
